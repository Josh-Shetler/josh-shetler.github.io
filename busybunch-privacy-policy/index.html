Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Busy Bunch: Family Planner ("the App") is a household planning application developed by Lowbit Labs (Joshua Shetler). The Android package name and the iOS bundle identifier are both com.lowbit.busybunch. This Privacy Policy describes the manner in which information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed in connection with your use of the App. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

Scope

This Policy applies solely to the Busy Bunch: Family Planner mobile application made available through the Apple App Store and Google Play. It does not apply to any third-party websites, services, or applications that may be linked from or accessible through the App, including Google, Firebase, AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play, which are governed by their own privacy practices.

Accounts and Google Sign-In

The App requires a Google account. When you sign in with Google Sign-In, the App receives your name, email address, profile photo, and Google account ID. That information is stored with Google Firebase Authentication and is used to identify your account and to associate you with a household. We do not collect a separate password, and we do not receive your Google account password.

You may revoke the App's access from your Google Account settings at any time. Revoking access may prevent you from signing in until you grant it again.

Households and Planner Data

You may create a household, which is assigned a join code, or join an existing household by entering its join code. A person who has the join code can become a member of that household. The household's planner data is stored in the cloud using Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, and is synced live between members of the same household.

Household planner data includes calendar events, chores, meals, grocery lists, family member names and colors, subtasks, and reminders settings. Only members of a household can access that household's planner data. We do not make household planner data available to other households or to the public.

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. Information about privacy in Firebase is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not delivered through a server-side push service operated by us. Reminder settings are part of the household planner data stored in Cloud Firestore so they can sync between members and devices. If you grant notification permission, the App uses it to show those local reminders. You may revoke notification permission at any time in your device system settings.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob and related Google advertising services may collect or receive device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address, and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable platform rules. The App requests non-personalized ads. The App does not track you across other companies' apps. Advertising identifiers may be reset or limited through your device settings where supported.

We do not sell your personal information. Google's advertising privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Payments and In-App Purchases

Busy Bunch Plus is offered as a subscription and as a lifetime purchase. These in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store billing, as applicable, and purchase status is managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an app user ID together with purchase and receipt information so that entitlements can be validated and restored. We do not collect, process, or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials. Those details are handled by Apple or Google.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at www.revenuecat.com/privacy. You may review the privacy policies of Apple and Google that apply to App Store and Google Play transactions. Managing or canceling a subscription is done in the Google Play or Apple account that was charged. Deleting your App account does not, by itself, cancel a store subscription.

No Sale of Data; No Analytics

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers. The App does not include an analytics SDK, and we do not use a third-party analytics service to measure how you use the App. We use the information described in this Policy to operate your account, sync a household, deliver local reminders, show advertisements, validate purchases, and respond to requests you send us.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account information and household planner data for as long as the account and household are in use and as needed to provide the App. You can leave a household, or request deletion of your account and the data associated with it, by emailing lowbit.support@gmail.com. Please send the request from the Google account you use with the App, or include that email address, so we can locate the correct records.

After we verify a deletion request, we delete the account information we control, including the name, email address, profile photo, and Google account ID stored for the App, together with household data we are able to remove for that request. If other people remain members of the household, shared planner data may stay available to them. If you want an entire household deleted, say so in the email and we will delete the household planner data we control. Limited records may be kept where we must do so to complete a purchase, comply with law, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements. Residual copies in routine backups are removed as those backups expire and are not used for any other purpose. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain purchase records under their own policies.

Uninstalling the App removes it from your device. Uninstalling does not, by itself, delete account or household data stored in Firebase.

Children

The App is intended for parents and caregivers who are 18 years of age or older. It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or caregiver may enter a child's name, and a color for that family member, as part of the household. Those entries are household planner data provided by the adult account holder. Children are not asked to create accounts. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us using the email address below and we will take reasonable steps to delete such information to the extent we have control over it.

Data Security

Household planner data is stored with Google Firebase and is available only to members of that household. The security of your account also depends on the security of your device and of the Google account you use to sign in. We rely on Firebase and the applicable app stores for storage, transmission, and access controls appropriate to this App. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Users

If you access the App from outside the United States, you understand that information processed by Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions that may have data-protection laws different from those of your country of residence.

Your Choices

You may: (a) revoke the App's Google Sign-In access in your Google Account settings; (b) leave a household, or request deletion of your account and associated data, by emailing lowbit.support@gmail.com; (c) decline or revoke notification permission in system settings; (d) limit advertising tracking or reset the advertising identifier where supported by your platform; (e) manage or cancel a Busy Bunch Plus subscription, or review a Busy Bunch Plus lifetime purchase, through Google Play or the Apple App Store; and/or (f) uninstall the App, which does not by itself delete cloud data stored for your account or household.

Your continued use of the App following the posting of changes constitutes your acknowledgment of the modified Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
