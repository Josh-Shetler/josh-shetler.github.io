Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

PixelGrip ("PixelGrip" or "the App") is a handheld game emulator for Android, published by Lowbit Labs. The package name is com.pixelgrip. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

PixelGrip does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. The App does not require an account, and it does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. Lowbit Labs does not operate a server for the App, and the App does not send your data to Lowbit Labs or to anyone else. Lowbit Labs does not sell your data.

Games and Files

PixelGrip does not include any commercial games. You choose game files to open with Android's system file picker. When you pick a file, the App copies it into its own private storage on your device so it can be played. These files are never uploaded.

Everything the App creates stays on your device, in storage that only PixelGrip can access:

  • Your game library and any cover images you add
  • In-game saves and save states, with small screenshot thumbnails for each save slot
  • Settings, such as theme, volume, vibration, and controller button mappings

Controller and touch input is used only to play the game and is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Backups

You can export your saves as a zip file, or import one, from Settings. The export is written only to a location you choose with Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud folder, such as Google Drive, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file. PixelGrip turns off Android's automatic app backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account by the App.

Advertising and Payments

The current version of PixelGrip shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. If a future version adds either, this policy will be updated first to describe what changes.

Permissions

The App declares Android's standard internet permission, which is included by the framework it is built on. PixelGrip does not use it to send or receive any of your data. The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored only on your device, you control it. To delete everything PixelGrip has stored, uninstall the App, or open Android Settings, tap Apps, then PixelGrip, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. Any backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Because the App collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
